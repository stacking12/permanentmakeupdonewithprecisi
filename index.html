FAQ

Common questions, answered

Permanent makeup is a form of cosmetic tattooing that deposits pigment into the skin's upper layers to mimic makeup — think filled-in brows, defined lips, or soft eyeliner that doesn't wash off. It's designed to fade gradually over one to three years rather than stay truly forever.
We apply topical numbing cream before and during your session, so most clients describe the sensation as light pressure or mild scratching rather than pain. Sensitivity varies by area and individual pain tolerance.
Most results last one to three years depending on your skin type, sun exposure, and skincare routine. Brows and lips fade first through the color, and a touch-up appointment refreshes shape and pigment.
Yes — PMU correction is one of our specialties. We assess faded, discolored, or unevenly shaped previous work and build a plan to soften, re-shape, or re-color it over one or more sessions.
